# Barr. Donald Chidi Amamgbo

**Category:** Law, Business, Oil & Gas, Politics, Philanthropy
**Type:** Individual

Nigerian lawyer (called to the Nigerian Bar 1985, California Bar 1993), business executive, oil and gas leader, politician and philanthropist from Ihiala, Anambra State. ADC candidate in the August 2025 Anambra South senatorial by-election and a sustained supporter of education, road construction and community empowerment initiatives.
